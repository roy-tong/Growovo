# Growovo: implementation status

**Release update, 2026-11-23 · [v0.19.0](../CHANGELOG.md).** Growovo now has a progressive modular runtime: correlated run/attempt/action ids, fail-loud atomic storage, canonical policy decisions, one-pass ledger indexes, recoverable local queue events and one scheduler state machine. Deterministic wave/receipt/readiness/KOC/business projections, signed inbox updates, B2B/warm/expert draft contracts and append-only reviewed feedback contributions share the same compatibility facade. Existing `review-ops`, Prove Anything export and all v0.18.1 X permission protections remain. The merged suite has 2242 passing offline tests; no real account sends or X writes are used.

**Release update, 2026-11-23 · [v0.18.1](../CHANGELOG.md).** All scripted non-API X browser writes fail closed, including one-off user-visible CDP and standing owner-attested sessions. Operator authorization and observed identity never substitute for platform permission. X read-only collection, dry runs, drafts and human handoff remain; automated writes require a separately connected, compliant official API. Public task pages now connect synthetic receipts to qualified replies, product use, buyer-owned next steps and second-use evidence without claiming external adoption.

Earlier baseline reviewed 2026-11-18. **Baseline on that date: v0.15.0 (delivery golden chain, platform strategy layer and grow v2).** Available means local implementation and local tests, not production validation on every platform or proof of business outcomes.

> Business architecture: [GTM 业务操作系统 v5](gtm-os/README.md) covers role families 07–46, work-order/SOP/deliverable/handoff contracts, KPI definitions and templates. R0/R1 are now locally implemented; see [implementation and remaining boundaries](gtm-os/05-implementation.md). Registry coverage is not equivalent to all 40 professional SOPs being executable or externally effective.

## External validation still required

- Full platform deep-research DoD is not complete. Registry size and document count cannot substitute for current official rules, practitioner methods, failure cases and platform-specific execution validation.
- Real account, proxy and authorized live-write tests are outside CI. They require separate authorization, platform/action/transport checks and readback evidence.
- Signal interpretation, opportunity and journey modules are deterministic reasoning aids, not proof that host semantics are true, demand exists, growth is causal, or revenue will follow.
- Local value-chain fixtures prove the state machine and real subprocess accounting only. External delivery receipts, user completion and production reliability still need authorized real-world verification.
- Experience counts and freshness are build-time facts and must be regenerated with the pipeline and status page. Previous counts such as 108/117 were snapshots, not permanent facts.
- Synthetic demos and local fixtures do not prove external business results, paid demand, production reliability or exact-once delivery.
