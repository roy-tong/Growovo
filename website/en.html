Why we built Growovo

We once sent 73 tweets in one night and gained 0 new stars. The useful lesson was not the zero. It was the product judgment behind it: send volume is not growth. Teams need a connected way to move from audience and content to replies and results.

FAQ

How is Growovo different from a scheduler?

A scheduler publishes. Growovo connects audience, content, execution, replies and results so the work after publication keeps moving too.

Who is Growovo for?

Independent developers, one-person companies and small teams that want a continuous growth practice. Start with one product facts pack and one small batch.

How do I start?

Run mkt demo to see a complete batch, then prepare your product facts, target audience and first growth goal.
