What it is

Give Growovo the product facts, audience and actions you authorize. It checks drafts, queues approved work and records receipts. An uncertain email send stops the batch for review. Track replies, activation and sales from their own records.

Full automation starts with platforms that can actually be automated. Phase one commits to unattended Email execution only. X non-API browser scripting is disabled; keep X read-only with drafts and manual handoff, or connect a compliant official API for automated writes. GitHub, Reddit, Hacker News and LinkedIn require a supervised first run; manual channels such as Xiaohongshu and Zhihu get work orders only. See the platform automation matrix.

The example shows both what it delivers and where it stops: one complete success path (gate → read-back → offline send → committed → daily result card) and three blocked runs (approval missing, placeholders present, read-back inconsistent). FAQ

Which platforms can it fully automate?

Phase one commits to unattended Email execution only. X non-API browser scripting is disabled; keep X read-only with drafts and manual handoff, or connect a compliant official API for automated writes. GitHub, Reddit, Hacker News and LinkedIn require a supervised first run. Manual channels such as Xiaohongshu and Zhihu get work orders only and never count toward automated volume. Check any time with mkt grow platforms.

Will it get my account banned?

It only operates accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to run. Sends go through authorization gates and a dry-run first; there are no bypasses around platform rules, and no promises about platform reach.

How is this different from a scheduling tool?

A scheduler publishes what you already wrote. Growovo starts from product facts and a growth goal — it sets strategy, builds the plan, executes, and records execution volume and daily readings in a verifiable ledger. The deliverable is a daily business result, not a queue of posts.

How do I verify the results?

Every committed action is in the ledger; daily readings are screenshot-backed with evidence grades; mkt demo runs the whole loop locally in about a second. Counter-examples are public — the launch autopsy above documents a real launch experiment that produced 0 new stars and never touched the ledger.

Does it cost money?

The tool is MIT open source with no paid tier. Local compute and model usage are billed by your own environment.

What happens when something fails?

Failures stop the loop for human adjudication; completion rates drive volume-change suggestions; supply gaps alert before they run out.
