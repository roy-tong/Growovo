What it is

Growovo is for indie developers and one-person companies — and for engineers who went full-stack and got handed GTM along with the codebase. You give it product facts and one growth goal; it sets the strategy, runs it, and delivers a daily business result: how many pieces went out, daily impressions, new followers. Strategy, plans and weekly reports are internal artifacts, not the deliverable.

Full automation starts with platforms that can actually be automated. Phase one commits to X + Email (verified write paths). GitHub, Reddit, Hacker News and LinkedIn are gated behind a supervised first run; manual channels such as Xiaohongshu and Zhihu get work orders only. See the platform automation matrix.

Exactly three verifiable commitments

CommitmentMeaning
Execution volumeevery committed action is in the ledger
Honest readingsdaily follower and impression snapshots, screenshot-backed and labelled by evidence grade
Closed-loop responsibilitycompletion drives volume changes, supply gaps alert early, failures stop the loop for human adjudication

It does not promise platform reach, and does not dress unverified channels up as full automation.

FAQ

Which platforms can it fully automate?

Phase one: X and Email only (X is PROVEN, Email is PORTED). GitHub, Reddit, Hacker News and LinkedIn need one supervised first run. Manual channels such as Xiaohongshu and Zhihu get work orders only and never count toward automated volume. Check any time with mkt grow platforms.

Will it get my account banned?

It only operates accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to run. Sends go through authorization gates and a dry-run first; there are no bypasses around platform rules, and no promises about platform reach.

How is this different from a scheduling tool?

A scheduler publishes what you already wrote. Growovo starts from product facts and a growth goal — it sets strategy, builds the plan, executes, and records execution volume and daily readings in a verifiable ledger. The deliverable is a daily business result, not a queue of posts.

How do I verify the results?

Every committed action is in the ledger; daily readings are screenshot-backed with evidence grades; mkt demo runs the whole loop locally in about a second. Counter-examples are public — the launch autopsy above documents a campaign that got 0 new stars.

Does it cost money?

The tool is MIT open source with no paid tier. Local compute and model usage are billed by your own environment.

What happens when something fails?

Failures stop the loop for human adjudication; completion rates drive volume-change suggestions; supply gaps alert before they run out.
